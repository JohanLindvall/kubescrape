# Code of conduct

kubescrape welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities and levels
of experience. Everyone participating in its issues, pull requests, reviews
and other project spaces is expected to help make those spaces respectful
and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with courtesy, including when you disagree.
- Discuss code, evidence and ideas without attacking the person behind them.
- Give constructive, specific feedback and allow people room to learn.
- Respect boundaries and privacy. Ask before sharing someone else's personal
  information or private communication.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention,
deliberate intimidation and publication of private information are not
acceptable. Repeated disruption or retaliation against someone who reports
a concern is also unacceptable.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the maintainer, Johan Lindvall, privately at
[johan.lindvall@gmail.com](mailto:johan.lindvall@gmail.com). Include links or
other relevant context and describe any immediate concern. Do not post
sensitive details in a public issue.

Reports will be handled with care for the privacy of those involved, with
information shared only as needed to address the situation. This contact is
for conduct concerns; report software vulnerabilities through the separate
[security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may request a change in behavior, edit or remove disruptive
content, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently, depending on
the severity and repetition of the conduct. Where appropriate, the affected
participant will receive an explanation and may provide additional context
through the same private contact.

These expectations also apply when someone represents kubescrape in a public
project event or another community space.
